Questions

How do I RSVP?

Use the RSVP button in your save-the-date email. It takes you straight to your party’s reservation.

Can I bring a guest or my kids?

Your invitation lists everyone in your party by name. We’ve made sure each person who is invited is listed. If you have any questions about your invitation, just reach out to us.

What should I wear?

We’ll share the dress code with the formal invitation. The celebration is outdoors among the redwoods, and October evenings in Saratoga can get cool, so bring a warm layer.

Is the venue indoors or outdoors?

Saratoga Springs is an outdoor venue set in the redwoods, with string lights overhead. Comfortable shoes for some uneven ground are a good idea.

Where should I stay?

We have a room block at the Juniper Hotel Cupertino, and the Inn at Saratoga is closest to everything. See Travel & stay for booking links.

Which airport should I fly into?

San José (SJC) is closest, about 25 minutes away. SFO is about 50 minutes and usually has the most flight options.

How do I reach you?

Email us at joinus@spencerandnikki.com. It goes straight to both of us.
